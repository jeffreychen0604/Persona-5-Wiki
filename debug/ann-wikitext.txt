This article covers information about the '''[[Lovers Arcana|Lovers]] [[Confidant]]''', '''[[Ann Takamaki]]''', including events and skills featured in ''[[Persona 5]]'' and ''[[Persona 5 Royal]]''.

The [[Ren Amamiya|protagonist]] automatically starts Ann's Confidant on April 15th, after she awakens to her [[Persona (concept)|Persona]] and the [[party]] has left [[Kamoshida's Palace]]. She can be found during the day in [[Shibuya]]'s Underground Mall.

Completing Ann's Confidant enables the fusion of [[Ishtar]]. She also gives the protagonist a '''[[Fashion Magazine]]''' on 3/19, which unlocks Sexy Technique♥ and Crocodile Tears once the protagonist establishes Ann's Confidant in [[New Game Plus]].
